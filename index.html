What is “Data”?

Anything that can be written down or recorded:
→ Your name
→ Your phone number
→ The price of a coffee
→ A photo of your dog
→ The date you bought something

What is an “Information System”?

Any organized way we store data so we can find it later and make smart decisions.

💥 The 9 Real Disasters That Happen When You Only Use Excel in a Real Business

1. Duplicate customers (Sarah appears 7 times)
2. Someone accidentally deletes row 4,347
3. Sheet becomes super slow after 8,000 rows
4. 5 people editing at the same time → chaos
5. Impossible to link “Customers” to “Orders” easily
6. No automatic validation (phone number with letters)
7. Can’t quickly answer “Who spent >$500 last month?”
8. Version confusion (which file is the real one?)
9. No backup → entire business can disappear

🗄️ What is a Database? (The simplest analogy ever)

Database = Smart Filing Cabinet

  • ✅ Separate drawers (tables)
  • ✅ Drawers can talk to each other
  • ✅ Locked and protected
  • ✅ Super fast search

Excel = One giant messy notebook

Everything on the same page → easy to lose things

Example: Coffee shop has 3 drawers → Customers • Products • Orders
